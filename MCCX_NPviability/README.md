As a coauthor, here I share same general information about this project to attract more attention from the community.

Authors: Qiankun Mo, Xingfei Wei, Ravithree Senanayake, Amelyn Phang, and Rigoberto Hernandez

MCCX: Combining Monte Carlo (MC) sampling, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), and Extreme Gradient-Boosting (XGB), as one such model in the context of learning and predicting the viability of organisms exposed to nanoparticles.

The strong performance of MCCX: Using a complex nonlinear neural network can maximize the utilization of every data point and extract every feature, verified in predicting viability of nanoparticles with a small experimental data set. This approach can achieve higher accuracy particularly for small data sets.
